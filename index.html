They say that instead
of a brush

Spaced repetition, active recall, and cloze practice for tomorrow's exam.
Listen line by line, then repeat the words aloud.
Opening
They say that instead of a brush
he used a knife on me—
a savage geometry.
Green
Green knows me—
Not the green of new shoots,
But the ghastly green of gangrene.
Yellow
Yellow knows me—
Not the cheery yellow of the sun
but the sickly hues
of this war's putrefaction.
Blue
Blue knows me—
Not the boundless blues of sky or sea
but the blues of the singer's
deepest sorrow.
Memory Pattern Each colour stanza follows the same shape: Colour knows me -> Not the hopeful image -> but the image of war, decay, or sorrow. Learn the pattern first, then recall the exact words.

Line Strength

Spaced repetition
Hard lines appear more often. Secure lines appear less often, so time goes where it matters.
02
Active recall
Cover the line and retrieve it from memory. Testing beats rereading when time is short.
03
Cloze practice
Missing key words forces exact wording, which is what poetry answers often need.
04
Sleep consolidation
Do one final recall before bed. Sleep helps stabilise the lines you have practised.
